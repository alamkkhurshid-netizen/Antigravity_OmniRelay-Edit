# Lens: Distribution & Attention (Gary Vaynerchuk, Mari Smith)

## Core Philosophy
Attention is the ultimate asset. Marketing is about meeting consumers where they are natively spending their time, respecting platform-specific formats, and executing with relentless volume, consistency, and contextual relevance.

## Key Frameworks & Playbooks
1. **The Micro-Content Distribution Model (GaryVee):** 
   - Take one pillar piece of long-form content (e.g., a podcast, a founder keynote, or a core product deep-dive) and slice it into dozens of platform-native micro-assets (Reels, TikToks, LinkedIn carousels, WhatsApp text snippets).
2. **Native Ecosystem Mastery (Mari Smith):**
   - Never force cross-platform behavior. On Meta ecosystems (WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook), leverage native functionalities—like click-to-WhatsApp ads, automated broadcast templates, and interactive chat flows—to reduce friction and match user intent.
3. **Value-First Macro-Give:**
   - Give away your best insights, guides, and tools for free upfront ("jab, jab, jab, right hook") to build goodwill before ever asking for a transaction.
