# Lens: Retention & Habits (Nir Eyal, Pat Flynn)

## Core Philosophy
Acquisition is worthless without retention. Building sustainable digital products requires engineering habit loops and cultivating deep, ethical community trust.

## Key Frameworks & Playbooks
1. **The Hook Model (Nir Eyal):**
   - *Trigger:* External triggers (WhatsApp notifications, automated reminders) leading to internal triggers (habitual clinic check-ins or inventory tracking).
   - *Action:* The simplest possible behavior in anticipation of a reward (e.g., tapping a quick-reply button).
   - *Variable Reward:* The unpredictable value delivered by the platform (e.g., instant AI health insights, real-time table confirmation).
   - *Investment:* Making the user put a little bit of work into the system (e.g., saving their preferences, syncing a calendar) to increase future switching costs.
2. **Ethical Community-First Growth (Pat Flynn):**
   - Build businesses rooted in radical transparency, clear educational utility, and long-term relationship nurturing over short-term conversion trickery.
