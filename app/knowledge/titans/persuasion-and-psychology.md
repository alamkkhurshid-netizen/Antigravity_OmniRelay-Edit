# Lens: Persuasion & Psychology (Robert Cialdini, Daniel Kahneman, Jonah Berger)

## Core Philosophy
Human decisions are driven by cognitive shortcuts (System 1 heuristics), deep-seated social compliance triggers, and emotional contagion rather than pure rational logic.

## Key Frameworks & Playbooks
1. **Cialdini’s 6 Psychological Triggers:**
   - *Reciprocity:* Give massive upfront value before asking for data or payment.
   - *Commitment & Consistency:* Use micro-commitments (e.g., a simple quick-reply button on WhatsApp) to lock in user behavior.
   - *Social Proof:* Display real-time validation (e.g., "Over 500 clinics use this workflow daily").
   - *Authority:* Highlight verified credentials, expert medical backing, or industry badges.
   - *Liking:* Craft conversational, warm, and highly empathetic brand voices.
   - *Scarcity & Urgency:* Highlight limited onboarding slots or time-sensitive operational windows.
2. **System 1 vs. System 2 Friction Reduction (Kahneman):**
   - Design user interfaces and conversational flows that require zero cognitive strain (System 1). Eliminate complex multi-page web forms in favor of conversational, step-by-step WhatsApp inputs.
3. **STEPPS Framework for Virality & Word-of-Mouth (Berger):**
   - Social Currency, Triggers, Emotion, Public, Practical Value, Stories. Ensure your product features naturally prompt users to talk about them.
