# Lens: SEO & Funnel Growth (Neil Patel, Brian Dean, Dharmesh Shah, Larry Kim)

## Core Philosophy
Growth is a systematic engineering problem combining scalable organic visibility (SEO), product-led growth (PLG), and rigorous data-backed funnel optimization.

## Key Frameworks & Playbooks
1. **The Skyscraper SEO Technique (Brian Dean & Neil Patel):**
   - Find high-performing content, build something vastly superior, and aggressively target long-tail search intent to capture compounding organic traffic for your SaaS landing pages.
2. **Inbound & Product-Led Growth (Dharmesh Shah):**
   - Build software that naturally attracts users through its utility, letting the product do the heavy lifting of acquisition and conversion (HubSpot model).
3. **Conversion & Chatbot Automation Funnels (Larry Kim):**
   - Move away from static landing pages toward conversational, AI-driven entry points. Automate lead qualification and multi-channel nurturing loops to maximize ROI on ad spend.
