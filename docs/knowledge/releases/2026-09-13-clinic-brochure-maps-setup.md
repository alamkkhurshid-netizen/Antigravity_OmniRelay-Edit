# Clinic brochure, Maps and provider setup — 13 September 2026

## What changed

- Business setup now has a compact doctor and staff profile layout.
- A clinic can either upload a public PDF brochure (maximum 10 MB) or save a public HTTPS brochure link.
- When a custom brochure is configured, WhatsApp option 5 opens it. Without one, OmniRelay generates the current public directory from active providers and clinic locations.
- Each location has a Google Maps share-link field. WhatsApp option 6 includes the configured location, address, active schedule and tappable Maps link.

## Safety boundary

- A brochure is public clinic information only. It must never contain a patient list, prescription, diagnosis, report, payment secret or staff private contact.
- Uploaded brochures are stored privately and made available through the public active-booking brochure route only.
- Live appointment availability remains separate from advertised hours and is confirmed during booking.
