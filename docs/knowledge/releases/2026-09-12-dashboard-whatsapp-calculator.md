# Dashboard WhatsApp calculator — 12 September 2026

## Product outcome

The OmniRelay home dashboard now has a **WhatsApp calculator** quick action. It opens a simple popup for appointment confirmations, appointment/care reminders and campaign recipients.

## What clinic owners should know

- The calculator is an estimate-planning aid, not an invoice, quote or wallet debit.
- It uses only a published, versioned Meta India rate card recorded in OmniRelay.
- If a verified rate card is not yet published, it explicitly says **Official rates pending** rather than showing an invented monetary amount.
- The calculator excludes SaaS subscription charges, GST treatment and AI Creative Studio services.
- Operational wallet charging and message blocking remain disabled during the clinic pilot.

## RAG impact

`guide_only`: the product-help catalogue now explains the dashboard calculator. No tenant document is automatically indexed and no customer-facing AI operation is activated.
