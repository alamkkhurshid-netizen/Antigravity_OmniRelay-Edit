## 1. Executive decision

Adopt **one OmniRelay brand, a shared platform fee, and vertical-specific packages**.

Every customer pays for the same reliable core: official WhatsApp, inbox, consent controls, templates, audit history, automation reliability and analytics. They then buy the workflow that produces their value: an OPD does not buy a generic chatbot; it buys fewer missed bookings, a managed queue and continued care. A restaurant buys occupied tables and repeat guests. A retailer buys recovered carts and faster order support.

This is better than one shared feature grid because a clinic should not have to understand catalogue or abandoned-cart terminology. It is better than completely separate products because OmniRelay retains one codebase, onboarding model, operational wallet and expansion path.

**Commercial rule:** never hide Meta or OmniRelay usage charges inside a vague âunlimited WhatsAppâ claim. Show a platform subscription plus a prepaid operational wallet, with a transaction ledger and pre-send estimate.

**Sell only Clinic packages until their pilot has passed.** Restaurant and Retail prices below are launch targets, not permission to market unfinished functionality.

### Operational wallet policy

OmniRelay pays Meta centrally, so the customer wallet must be used before a business-initiated message is released. Preserve a small safety reserve and stop non-urgent sends if it is insufficient. Appointment safety messages may be queued for staff action rather than silently discarded.

Use the current Meta India rate card as the source of base cost, version it in the admin panel, and calculate customer price at send time. Meta has published changes effective **1 October 2026**, including charges for service messages in the customer-service window; do not use the older âservice is freeâ assumption. [Meta pricing documentation](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing) and [Meta service-message update](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/non-template-messages) are controlling sources.

**Markup policy (customer-visible aggregate price, not hidden):**

| Category | Why | OmniRelay margin rule |
|---|---|---|
| Utility and authentication | Directly supports booked visits/orders and automation reliability | Volume-sensitive: 25% at low usage, decreasing to 10% at high usage |
| Marketing | Must not reward indiscriminate broadcasting | Narrow band: 22% at low usage, decreasing only to 18% at high usage |
| Inbound customer messages | No Meta outbound template charge; product access is covered by subscription | No per-message OmniRelay charge |

Rates shown to a customer must always state **rate-card version, included/excluded GST, category and delivery basis**. The wallet/ledger system remains **shadow-mode only** until a full clinic month reconciles to Meta and Razorpay records.

### Why OmniRelay: the customer-facing Triple-W

**The competitive truth:** established providers are capable horizontal WhatsApp platforms. They provide an API, inbox, campaigns and generic builders. OmniRelay must never claim that they cannot send messages, create chatbots or integrate WhatsApp. Our edge is that OmniRelay starts with the operating workflow and makes the next action obvious for a real clinic, then repeats that model for other verticals.

| Customer question | OmniRelay answer | Why a generic platform is different |
|---|---|---|
| **Why should I buy it?** | To stop valuable conversations from becoming missed calls, no-shows, manual follow-ups and invisible operational leakage. | A generic platform gives tools; the business must still design, maintain and train staff on the workflow. |
| **What exactly do I get?** | A ready-to-run vertical operating layer: booking/reservation/order flow, approved message controls, inbox handoff, staff actions, consent evidence and owner visibility. | Typically an inbox, campaign tool, bot/flow builder and integrations. | **Whose problem does it solve?** | Owner: visibility and recovery. Reception/team: fewer repetitive calls and clear next steps. Customer/patient: faster, relevant communication and easy booking/help. | Generic software mainly serves the administrator or marketing operator who configures it. |

### OmniRelay's defendable edge

1. **Workflow product, not tool access.** Clinic Operations is designed around the OPD day: doctor/chamber schedules, booking, payment choice, confirmation, 24h/2h reminder, reschedule/cancel, continued-care planning, queue operations and doctor-specific emergency handling. This removes the âbuild it yourselfâ burden.
2. **One customer journey, one source of truth.** WhatsApp conversation, appointment, consent, payment state, follow-up and owner dashboard are connected. A campaign or reminder is not an isolated blast from a marketing panel.
3. **Operational safety is part of the product.** Consent-first sending, approved Meta templates, delivery/audit records, role-aware actions, human handoff and balance protection are core controlsânot premium decoration.
4. **Vertical depth with shared economics.** The platform core stays common, but templates, data model, dashboards and recommended actions change by vertical. This gives customers a specialist experience without locking OmniRelay into one market.
5. **Evidence before AI claims.** RAG assistants must answer from approved tenant knowledge, cite or hand off when uncertain, and never invent a medical, pricing or policy answer. The owner sees operational evidence, not an opaque âAI score.â
6. **Transparent commercial model.** Subscription is for the product; the wallet is for actual variable usage. A clinic can see estimate, deduction and remaining balance instead of guessing why its bill increased.
